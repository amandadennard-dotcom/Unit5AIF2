SDG 6: Clean Water and Sanitation. Target 6.1: Safe and affordable drinking water

Community Issue

Some of the drinking fountains at our school taste strange or look cloudy. Students stop drinking from them, and some go without water during the day. The school tests the water only a few times a year, so nobody can tell right away when a fountain has a problem.

Our System Idea

  • Our system checks the water at each fountain all day and tells us when something looks wrong.
  • It uses sensors and AI to compare new readings to what is normal for that fountain.
  • It shows a status light on the fountain and sends the results to a dashboard, so school staff know which fountain to check.

How We Use Data

GoalFind out when a fountain's water might not be safe, so someone can check it quickly.
DataCloudiness and pH readings from each fountain, plus the time of each reading.
ActionCompare. The AI compares new readings to what is normal for that fountain.
OutputA status for each fountain: OK or Check water.
InsightWhich fountains have problems, and when. For example, we might find that water gets cloudy after long breaks.

Where students make this: In student Level 4 on Day 3, as a reflection based on the peer feedback they get during the gallery walk.

Ethical Considerations

First, we only collect water readings. We do not collect any information about people, so nobody's privacy is at risk.

Second, the AI could be wrong if it only learned from one fountain. We would test it on fountains in every building so it works for the whole school.

Third, we want students to trust the results. The status light is on the fountain where everyone can see it, and we would explain how the AI makes its decision.

Our system does not replace official water tests. In the future, we would ask water experts to check our readings and ask students and staff for feedback.

Where students make this: In student Level 5 on Day 3. They import their system diagram from the backpack into AI Chat, ask for feedback, and complete the AI as a Co-Creator section of their project file.

AI as Co-Creator

We asked AI for feedback on our system diagram.

A suggestion we accepted

AI said students should be able to see the water status too, not only school staff. We accepted this and added the status light on the fountain.

A suggestion we rejected

AI also suggested adding a sensor for lead and other metals. We rejected this because those sensors are expensive and too complicated for our design. Official school testing should still check for those.

AI gave us useful ideas, but we still had to decide which ones made sense for our project.
